2 · Naming files

A good name says which project, what it is and when, without opening the file.

Rules

  • Three parts, always in this order: project, purpose, date.
  • Pick one style and use it everywhere. Our default is the easiest to read.
  • Date as year-month-day, so files sort correctly.
  • No spaces. No “final”, “new” or “copy”.
