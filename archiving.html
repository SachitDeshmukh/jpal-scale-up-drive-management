3 · Archiving

Archiving keeps live folders clean without losing history. File by project or theme, then year.

When to archive

  • A project phase or grant has closed.
  • A newer version replaces the file.
  • Nobody has opened it for 12 months.
  • Never archive the only copy of a live template.

How to archive

  1. Keep one version; delete duplicates.
  2. Move it to 4_Archive, under its project or theme and year.
  3. Add a line to 0_README.
